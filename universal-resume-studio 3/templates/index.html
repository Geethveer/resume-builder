RSResume Studio
Private local prototype v0.1
WORKFLOW

Make every
application count.

One verified profile. Three screening gates. No made-up experience.

Privacy by design

Runs on your computer. Resume data is not sent to an external AI service. Saving your profile is optional. This prototype is not a public multi-user service.

EVIDENCE FIRST

Build an application from facts, not fiction.

Complete the short intake once. Upload a resume, paste a job description, and check eligibility before creating an ATS-readable application package.

01 / 05

Your profile

Only your name and resume are required to begin. Other details improve document quality and job matching.

02 / 05

Eligibility & goals

These details are used only to evaluate job fit and eligibility. Do not enter passport numbers, immigration case numbers, or identification documents.

03 / 05

Your evidence

Upload your current resume. Supplement it with achievements that you can personally verify. Nothing will be invented or assigned to an employer without your evidence.

04 / 05

Target opening

Paste the full posting including minimum qualifications and responsibilities. A job URL alone is not enough for an accurate screen.

Optional: employer research evidence and H-1B records

For U.S. applications, historical H-1B approvals provide context but not a promise to sponsor this role. E-Verify enrollment is a separate check.

05 / 05

Verification results

Nothing is generated until all gates clear. Gaps need your explicit approval, and a baseline alignment score of 65 or lower stops the process.

Complete the questionnaire and analyze a job to see the gates.

Scores are internal alignment heuristics, not actual scores from employer ATS products. Review all generated text before applying.
