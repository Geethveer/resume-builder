Senior Manager, Supply Planning
Example Distribution Co.

Responsibilities
- Lead inventory and supply planning across distribution branches and supplier operations.
- Develop capacity plans, forecast demand, coordinate purchase orders, and monitor shipments.
- Assess supplier risks, recommend recovery actions, and communicate risk to leadership teams.
- Improve supplier performance, execute replenishment plans, and support order management.

Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor degree in business, engineering, or supply chain management.
- 5+ years of experience in purchasing, forecasting, planning, logistics, or inventory control.
- Experience leading supplier performance and solving supply chain disruptions.
- Experience analyzing data in Excel and managing cross-functional stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications
- Power BI reporting and supply chain process improvement.
